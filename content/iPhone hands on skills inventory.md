> [!note]
> The outline on this page is my attempt to create an inventory of the things I think anyone/everyone would want/need to do when using an iPhone at some point.  I.e., this is an attempt to itemize the "essential" kinds of things anyone/everyone does.  And yes, it turns out to be a lot of things!
> 
> The primary question I am interested in your feedback on at this point is **how many of these things do you already consider yourself familiar with and comfortable doing?**
> 
> For our Oct 30 2026 meeting, we can limit our focus to sections 1 through 5 below; the other sections will likely benefit from some additional refinement by me and after I get initial feedback on sections 1 through 5.
> 
> So...as you look at the details of sections 1 through 5, ask yourself which of the following most closely describes your thought on each skill:
> 
> 1. I know how to do this; I don't need/want to spend much if any time drilling into it further in a meeting.
> 2. I know what this is referring to but am uncertain about how to do it and would like to learn more.
> 3. I don't understand what this item is referring to (never mind how to do it.)

# 0. Key Concepts & UI Constructs

> [!note]
> I referenced a number of sources while pulling this inventory together and noticed recurring references to some things that likely need some contextual explanation; I am keeping track of those things in this section. 

1. Lock Screen
2. Home Screen
3. Control Center
4. Notification Center
5. App Library
6. Spotlight
7. Settings: review of key differences between iPhone/iPad and Mac
8. App Sandbox -- pertains to how the iPhone (and iPad for that matter) manage files (and why/how that is different than how files are managed on Macs)
# 1. General

1. You can turn your iPhone on and off.
	1. You can "force restart" your iPhone.
2. You can lock, unlock and wake your iPhone
3. You can find key information about your iPhone: model name, serial number, type of process, amount of memory and storage, and the version of the OS your iPhone is using
4. You can "read" the icons in your iPhone's status bar
5. You can adjust the volume of your iPhone's speakers
6. You can use the "Screen Time" to review how you are spending time when using your iPhone
7. You can sign in and out of your Apple account
8. You can keep the version of software on your device (i.e., the OS and apps) up to date
9. Picture in Picture 
10. You can create screenshots and/or record the screen
11. Basic customizations
	1. You can increase/decrease the size of text and other onscreen content
	2. You can turn Dark Mode on and off (to reduce eyestrain in low-light environments)
	3. You can change the date and time
	4. You can change the name of your iPhone
	5. You can configure accounts the Mail, Contacts, and Calendar apps use
	6. You can change the apps your iPhone uses by default when opening files and links
	7. You can change the internet search engine your iPhone uses by default
	8. You can personalize your Lock Screen by choosing a wallpaper, showcasing a favorite photo, changing the font of the time, adding widgets, etc. 
# 3. Managing WiFi and cellular network connections

1. You can view and change your cellular data settings
2. You can determine if your iPhone is connected to a cellular network
3. You can turn your iPhone's cellular network connection on and off
4. You can determine if you iPhone is connected to a WiFi network
5. You can turn your iPhone's WiFi connection on and off
6. You know how to "tell" your iPhone to "remember" and "forget" a WiFi network
# 4. Managing storage

1. You can check storage (both local and iCloud)
2. You can control when a file is saved to local storage or iCloud storage
3. You can remove items to increase the amount of available storage
4. You can offload (i.e., remove) apps (with or without retaining data they manage)
# 5. Accessing and managing apps

1. You can find apps you have downloaded/installed
	1. Using the App Library
2. You can install and remove (uninstall) apps
3. You can open and quit apps
4. You can switch between open apps
5. You can find an apps settings
6. You can access & use Quick Action menus
7. You can rearrange app icons on your Home Screen
# 6. Search: finding things on your iPhone

1. You can use Spotlight to find apps, files, messages (email and text), etc stored on your iPhone
# 7. Notifications

1. You can change the sounds iPhone plays when you get a call, message, and alert.
2. You can turn on/off vibrations (i.e., haptics)
3. find, view, dismiss, clear, respond 
4. turn-off and mute *for an app*

- You can "silence" your iPhone when you don't want to be disturbed/distracted by alerts/"chimes"/notifications
- Personalize your iPhone > Set up Focus, notifications, and Do Not Disturb
	- Focus
	- Do Not Disturb (I think I saw a mention that indicated there is a DND feature separate/distinct from Focus)
# 8. Working with text

- Using the virtual keyboard
	- Predictive text
	- Shorthand for frequently used text
- Select, edit, cut/paste, and move text
- Looking up the definition of words/phrases
- Translating text in other languages to English
- Work with text and graphics > Use Markup tools
	- Draw and handwrite with Markup
	- Add text, shapes, stickers, and more
- Work with text and graphics > Fill out forms, sign documents, and create signatures

# 11. Privacy

1. Control what you share

	2. Control app tracking permissions
	3. Control the location information you share
	4. Control access to information in apps

	6. Control how Apple delivers advertising to you
	7. Control access to hardware features
