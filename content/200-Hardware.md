# Aims

1. Provide an overview of the anatomy of computational devices
2. Define the roles of--and relationship between--a device's processor, memory, and storage
3. Explain how a device's processor(s), memory, and storage contained work together as the lowest-level **computational engine** of digital technology
4. Describe how devices can be connected to each other
5. Summarize the philosophy/principles of human-computer interaction 
6. Explain how the computational power and capacity of computational devices is measured
# Objectives

1. You can find your device's make/model/serial number, age, and processor type
2. You can describe your device's processing power/capacity
3. You can identify the amount of your device's storage that is free/available for use
4. You can review how much storage is currently being used by apps
5. You can cleanup/reclaim/optimize your device's storage when needed
6. You can determine when your device is connected to the Internet and when it is not
7. You can manage when/how your device connects to WiFi networks
8. You can perform the basic pointing device gestures using a mouse, trackpad, and touchscreen
# Scope

1. Overview
2. Processors, memory, and storage
3. Interactivity
	1. Input: keyboard, pointing device, camera, microphone
	2. Output: screen, speakers, haptics
	3. Gestures: an introduction to pointing devices and their use
4. Connectivity
	1. Wired vs wireless
	2. Ports
	3. Peripherals
	4. Internet access: WiFi vs cellular
5. Measuring the computational/processing power of computers
	1. Measuring the speed of processors
	2. Measuring the capacity of memory and storage
	3. Moore's Law
	4. Device "t-shirt" sizes
6. The lifespan of computational devices
