# Aims

The Internet is the foundational technology that enables us to do things "online." It is a distributed repository of human knowledge and services, and enables near-real time communication with other people globally. 

This section describes how the Internet works using the concepts/constructs introduced in the hardware and software sections of the course.
# Objectives

- You can "read" a URL and identify its associate site and type of resource
- You can manage your browser's cache
- You can differentiate between static and dynamic websites
- You are able to examine a site's connection security details using your browser
- You can inspect an email for indications of scam/phishing/malicious threats
- You can organize your email with folders and reduce inbox "clutter" using common email service features (e.g., blocking and filters)
